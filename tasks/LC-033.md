## Details
Turn the November trip into a structured feasibility/origination mission. Stakeholder list exists; now it needs to become an actual programme.

## Stakeholders to pursue
- ADB project/budget owner and the second ADB sponsor you need;
- Sarvodaya;
- Dialog;
- ideally the other telcos as well rather than treating one operator as the whole market;
- TRC;
- Disaster Management Centre;
- relevant power-sector entities;
- tower/infrastructure owners;
- local disaster-response providers;
- Vodafone Foundation/INER where useful;
- satellite/connectivity providers.

## Also
Ask your existing ADB contact to get you into the relevant team/budget holder even if their internal structure is changing.

## Interview questions to answer
1. Who actually owns the relevant risk?
2. Who is the beneficiary?
3. Who pays the premium?
4. Could donors fund/subsidise it?
5. Who owns the towers/assets?
6. What happens operationally today when a cyclone hits?
7. What is required before, during and immediately after the event?
8. What equipment/services are scarce?
9. Can generators, satellite capacity, repair crews etc. be pre-contracted?
10. What training/readiness could be funded in advance?
11. What data exists for triggers?
12. What does restoration actually cost?
13. What should Lionscraft protect: towers, connectivity availability, power continuity, restoration time, public-service outcomes, or some combination?

## Output
That becomes the feasibility study -> facility design.
